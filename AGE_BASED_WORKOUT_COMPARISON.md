## Common Mistakes by Age Group

### Age 20-25 ❌
- Push too hard too fast (risk overuse injury)
- Skip warm-up (cause sudden joint stress)
- Neglect flexibility (future problems)

### Age 26-35 ❌
- Try to train like 20-year-olds (recovery slower)
- Ignore form for heavier weight
- Insufficient sleep

### Age 36-50 ❌
- **Downgrade intensity way too much** (can handle moderate)
- Skip recovery sessions
- Too much strength focus (need cardio!)

### Age 51-65 ❌
- **Try to match younger people's intensity** (injury risk!)
- Do heavy compound lifts without form check
- Rush recovery between sets

### Age 65+ ❌
- **Start too intensely** (must be VERY gentle)
- Exercise alone without supervision
- Push through pain
- Skip warm-up

## When to STOP & Consult Doctor

🔴 **STOP IMMEDIATELY IF YOU EXPERIENCE:**
- Chest pain or pressure
- Shortness of breath (not from exertion)
- Dizziness or fainting
- Sharp joint pain (not muscle soreness)
- Sudden weakness or numbness
- Any pain that doesn't go away after rest
